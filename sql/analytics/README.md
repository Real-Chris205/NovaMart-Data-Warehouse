# Business Analytics SQL Scripts

This directory contains SQL queries developed to analyse the NovaMart Data Warehouse and generate business insights from the warehouse data.

## Analytical Queries

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `01-business-analytical-queries.sql` | Contains business analytical queries for monthly revenue, product revenue, store revenue, and category revenue analysis. |

## Analysis Areas

The analytical queries cover:

- Monthly revenue analysis.
- Product revenue analysis.
- Store revenue analysis.
- Category revenue analysis.

## Analytical Results

The queries were used to validate business performance across the warehouse:

- Total revenue: ₦302,918.00.
- Highest revenue-generating product: Xiaomi SU7 Ultra.
- Highest revenue-generating store: Sports.
- Highest revenue-generating category: Cars.

## Purpose

These queries demonstrate how the dimensional data warehouse can support business reporting and help identify revenue trends and top-performing products, stores, and categories.
